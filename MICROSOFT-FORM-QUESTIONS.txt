Grow With Cousins - Interest Form

Description: Tell us what interests you so we can start a career conversation.

1. First and last name - required text
2. Store number or location - required text
3. Current position - required single choice:
   Crew Member; Crew Leader; Shift Manager; Assistant Manager;
   General Manager; Other
4. Position or opportunity you are interested in - required single choice:
   Crew Leader; Shift Manager; Assistant Manager; General Manager;
   Certified Training General Manager; Area Director;
   Corporate Support Center Roles; I am not sure yet
5. What would you like to learn more about? - optional long text
6. Who would you feel most comfortable speaking with first? - optional choice:
   My General Manager; My Area Director; Human Resources; No preference
7. Acknowledgment - required acceptance:
   I understand that submitting this form starts a career conversation.
   It does not guarantee training, promotion, a position opening or a
   specific timeline. My information may be shared with Human Resources,
   my General Manager and my Area Director.

Privacy note: Do not enter employee IDs, passwords, Social Security numbers,
medical information or other sensitive information.

Thank-you message: Thank you for raising your hand. Human Resources will review
your interest and help connect you with the appropriate restaurant leader.
Continue talking with your manager and working on your current training.
