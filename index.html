What changes as you grow

Open a position to see the expectations, training and opportunities connected to it.

01Crew MemberLearn the stations+
You BecomeA dependable team member.

Next potential step: Crew Leader

What You Learn

  • Core restaurant stations
  • Welcoming guests, taking accurate orders and making sure they are cared for
  • Food quality and safety
  • Cleanliness and restaurant standards
  • Dependability and teamwork

Training

  • Phase 1 training
  • Station training and skills verification
  • Additional cross-training based on restaurant needs

What You Gain

  • Free sandwich during each shift
  • Next Day Pay
  • 50% discount while off duty
  • Health, dental and vision options for eligible employees
  • 401(k) with company match and pet insurance
  • Discounted college courses and scholarship opportunities
  • Employee wellness, assistance and ongoing development programs
02Crew LeaderHelp the team+
You BecomeSomeone teammates look to for help.

Next potential step: Shift Manager

What You Demonstrate

  • Strong performance at multiple stations
  • Dependability and follow-through
  • Positive peer support
  • Leading by example by putting guests first
  • Ability to help train and guide teammates
  • Consistent food safety, quality and service standards

Training

  • Phase 2 training
  • Advanced station verification
  • Leadership practice and peer support

What You Gain

  • All eligible Crew Member benefits
  • Free sandwich and side during each shift
  • Increased pay with promotion
  • Additional leadership training and experience
03Shift ManagerLead the shift+
You BecomeA leader who runs successful shifts.

Next potential step: Assistant Manager

What You Demonstrate

  • Ability to lead opening and closing shifts
  • Clear communication and delegation
  • Calm decision-making
  • Coaching and accountability
  • Keeping the team focused on guests and responding to their needs and concerns
  • Strong shift readiness, service and food safety
  • Dependable attendance and follow-through

Training

  • Phase 3 training
  • Required manager training
  • Opening, closing, cash-handling and shift-leadership verification

What You Gain

  • All eligible Crew Member and Crew Leader benefits
  • Free meal during each shift
  • PTO eligibility based on current company policy
  • Increased pay with promotion
  • Manager training and leadership experience
04Assistant ManagerHelp run the restaurant+
You BecomeA trusted second-in-command preparing to become a General Manager.

Next potential step: General Manager

What You Demonstrate

  • Ability to run the restaurant when the GM is unavailable
  • Employee coaching and development
  • Coaching the team to provide welcoming, accurate and attentive guest service
  • Hiring and onboarding support
  • Scheduling and staffing awareness
  • Inventory, food cost and labor understanding
  • Strong organization, decision-making and position availability

Training

  • Phase 4 training
  • Manager Training Program
  • Hands-on practice with restaurant administration and business operations

What You Gain

  • All eligible Crew Member, Crew Leader and Shift Manager benefits
  • Ability to work up to 45 hours or more based on business needs, with overtime eligibility
  • Tips and participation in the bonus program
  • Average additional earnings of $4,000 or more annually through tips and bonuses
  • Paid time off, paid holidays and a free meal during each shift
  • Experience preparing to become a General Manager

Tips and bonus earnings vary by restaurant, hours worked, performance and program eligibility. Average or past earnings are not guaranteed.

05General ManagerLead the restaurant+
You BecomeA business leader responsible for an entire restaurant.

Next potential step: CTGM, Area Director or Corporate Support Center Roles

What You Lead

  • Restaurant staffing, culture and employee development
  • Scheduling and labor management
  • Food cost, inventory, ordering and variance
  • A 100% guest-focused culture where every guest feels welcomed and cared for
  • Food safety and compliance
  • Restaurant performance and action planning
  • Development of future leaders

Training

  • General Manager training
  • Required workshops and certifications
  • ServSafe certification
  • Business, leadership and operational development

What You Gain

  • Salaried restaurant leadership experience
  • Bonus opportunity based on the current program
  • Responsibility for a full restaurant operation
  • Opportunity to develop future leaders
  • Eligibility for future leadership opportunities

Benefits, compensation, training requirements and program eligibility are subject to current company policies and may change.

A clear process

How promotions work

Growth starts with strong performance today and a conversation about what comes next.

  1. 1
    Learn Your Current Role

    Build strong skills, take care of guests and consistently meet the expectations of your current position.

  2. 2
    Share Your Interest

    Tell your manager or open the interest form using the button below.

  3. 3
    Build Your Skills

    Complete training and practice the responsibilities needed for the next role.

  4. 4
    Demonstrate Readiness

    Show consistent performance, leadership, dependability and accountability while keeping guests first.

  5. 5
    Move Forward

    Promotions depend on manager recommendation, required approvals, an available opportunity and business need.

Employees can continue developing even when there is not an immediate opening. Expressing interest or completing training does not guarantee promotion.

Go beyond one restaurant

Future opportunities

Restaurant leadership can open several paths across Cousins Subs.

Certified Training GM+

Certified Training General Managers lead successful, guest-focused restaurants while teaching newly hired and promoted managers how to take care of their teams and guests.

  • Official promotion with a pay change
  • Area Director nomination and Training Store standards required
  • Minimum 90% audit score for nomination
  • 93% with no food safety failures for final certification
  • Final approval from the Training Department
  • Designation is connected to the GM and certified restaurant
  • A transferring CTGM must reapply and certify the new location
Area Director+

Area Directors lead and support multiple restaurants, develop General Managers, and help drive operational and business performance while keeping teams focused on taking care of every guest.

Corporate Support Center Roles+

Restaurant experience can help prepare employees for available positions in Training, Operations, Human Resources, Marketing, IT, Finance, Maintenance and other support functions. These teams support our restaurants so employees can take great care of our guests.

Frequently asked questions

Does submitting the form guarantee a promotion?+

No. It starts a conversation about your interests and development.

Do I need to wait for a position to open?+

No. You can build skills and prepare even when there is not an immediate opening.

How long does promotion take?+

There is no standard timeline. It depends on your experience, training, performance, readiness, restaurant needs and available opportunities.

What happens after I submit the form?+

Human Resources will review your submission and share it with the appropriate restaurant leaders so a career conversation can begin.

Who will see my information?+

Human Resources and the appropriate General Manager and Area Director may review your submission.

Can I change my mind?+

Yes. Expressing interest does not commit you to accepting training or a future position.

Where does my training tracker fit?+

Your existing training tracker remains the official tool for recording operational training and skill completion.
